# Changelog: linkedin-hook-score

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-04)
First release. Scores each drafted hook from 1 to 10 against your own best posts, with one plain reason, in a band above the prompt. Learns from LinkedIn's creator analytics export (.xlsx), the "Get a copy of your data" archive (Shares.csv) or a top-posts.md you paste into. Uses your own Claude (haiku by default). Needs Claude Code 2.1.287 or newer.
